## 🌍 Overview

This project focused on the **modelling and analysis of road network accessibility and electric vehicle (EV) charging coverage at the European scale**, carried out at the **Joint Research Centre (JRC) of the European Commission**.

The objective was twofold:

1. **Assess accessibility to services and population** through the road infrastructure.
2. **Evaluate the spatial coverage of electric vehicle charging stations** with respect to current and future EU transport policies.

The work supported **evidence-based transport policy and infrastructure planning** across EU member states.

---

## 🗺️ Methods & Technologies

- Large-scale **graph construction** from TomTom and geospatial road datasets  
- **Accessibility modelling** and service reachability analysis  
- **Multicriteria spatial analysis**  
- **Geospatial data integration and harmonisation**  
- **Electric vehicle charging coverage analysis**  
- Generation of **isochrones, heatmaps, and accessibility surfaces**

**Tools & Languages:**  
C++ · Python · MATLAB · QGIS · GeoPandas · Graph Algorithms · Geospatial Data Processing

---

## ⚙️ My Role

- Built **large-scale road network graphs** from heterogeneous geospatial sources  
- Performed **spatial accessibility analysis** for:
  - Health services
  - Education facilities
  - Population reachability
- Analysed **EV charging infrastructure coverage** to identify underserved areas  
- Integrated multiple datasets into a **unified analytical framework**  
- Produced **visual outputs** such as:
  - Accessibility heatmaps
  - Travel-time isochrones
- Collaborated with **multidisciplinary policy and modelling teams** at EU level

---

## 📈 Outcomes & Impact

- Quantified **service accessibility gaps** across multiple European regions  
- Identified **critical areas for EV charging infrastructure deployment**  
- Provided **decision-support maps and indicators** to EU policymakers  
- Contributed to **sustainable transport planning and green mobility strategies**
