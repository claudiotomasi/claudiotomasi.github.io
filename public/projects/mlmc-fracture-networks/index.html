Multilevel Monte Carlo for Random Fracture Networks

monte-carlo uncertainty-quantification geophysics numerical-simulation

🌍 Overview

This project was developed during my double Master’s degree and focused on the simulation of fluid flow in random underground fracture networks for geothermal energy feasibility assessment.

The key challenge is the strong geometrical uncertainty in fracture configurations, which makes standard Monte Carlo simulations computationally expensive.

To address this, the project employed Multilevel Monte Carlo (MLMC) techniques to significantly reduce computational cost while preserving statistical accuracy.

🧪 Methods & Technologies

  • Stochastic modeling of fracture networks
  • Multilevel Monte Carlo simulation
  • Finite element / finite volume discretization
  • Parallel numerical simulation
  • Statistical error control

Tools & Languages:
Python · MATLAB · C++ · Numerical Simulation · High-Performance Computing

⚙️ My Role

  • Implemented Monte Carlo and MLMC simulation frameworks
  • Generated large ensembles of random fracture networks
  • Performed uncertainty quantification on flow and permeability
  • Analysed computational complexity and convergence behavior
  • Produced validation and comparison studies

📈 Outcomes

  • Significant reduction of computational cost through MLMC
  • Quantification of uncertainty in geothermal flow predictions
  • Master’s thesis submitted with full numerical validation
  • Results used for feasibility studies in geothermal contexts

🏷️ Research Areas

Uncertainty Quantification · Monte Carlo Methods · Geothermal Engineering · Numerical Simulation
